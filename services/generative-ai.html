Legacy System Integration

How it works

Our generative AI integration process.

Every integration engagement follows a structured six-phase process, from discovery through continuous optimization, ensuring production-ready results with measurable business impact.

01 Discovery

Discovery & infrastructure assessment

We analyze your applications, APIs, databases, workflows, and compliance requirements to determine where generative AI creates measurable business value.

02 Architecture

Integration architecture design

Our engineers design the complete enterprise AI architecture, including API integrations, MCP connectors, model orchestration, RAG pipelines, and governance controls.

03 Development

AI integration development

We develop secure integrations between AI models and enterprise applications while building scalable middleware, authentication, and monitoring.

04 Validation

Testing & validation

Every integration undergoes functional testing, security validation, retrieval accuracy evaluation, performance testing, and load testing before deployment.

05 Deployment

Production deployment

We deploy AI integrations with role-based access control, monitoring, logging, rollback strategies, and operational documentation.

06 Optimization

Continuous optimization

After launch, we monitor model performance, latency, AI usage, operational costs, and retrieval quality to continuously improve business outcomes.
