FAQ’s

### **What's the difference between adding AI features and AI SaaS development?**

AI SaaS development designs your platform around AI from the beginning, including multi-tenant architecture, model routing, usage tracking, and scalability. Adding AI features typically connects a model to an existing application without addressing the underlying platform architecture.

### **Can you integrate AI into our existing SaaS platform instead of rebuilding?**

Yes. We first evaluate your existing architecture to determine whether AI capabilities can be integrated or whether certain components need to be redesigned for performance, scalability, or security.

### **Do we own the AI models and code after launch?**

Yes. You receive full ownership of the source code, infrastructure configurations, model integrations, and deployment assets. There is no vendor lock-in, giving you complete control over your platform.

### **How do you control AI infrastructure costs as usage grows?**

We build cost optimization into the platform through model routing, response caching, usage monitoring, and cost-per-inference analytics, helping you scale without unexpected infrastructure expenses.

### **How long does an AI SaaS MVP take?**

Most AI-native MVPs are delivered within 10 to 16 weeks, depending on feature complexity, AI integrations, multi-tenant requirements, and any custom model training or fine-tuning involved.
