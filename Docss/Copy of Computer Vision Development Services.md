## **Computer Vision Development Services We Provide**

Every solution is designed around your business data, infrastructure, and operational requirements, not generic APIs or off-the-shelf models.

#### **Object Detection and Recognition**

Localizes and classifies people, products, vehicles, or equipment in real time from live camera feeds or stored footage. Powers counting, tracking, and automated alerts across manufacturing, logistics, and retail floor environments.

#### **Image Classification and Tagging**

Sorts large image libraries into categories automatically, replacing manual review queues. Useful for content moderation, catalog management, and any workflow where thousands of images need consistent labeling fast.

#### **Video Analytics and Monitoring**

Analyzes streaming video for behavioral patterns, occupancy levels, and anomalies as they happen. Triggers event-based alerts instead of relying on someone watching a monitor bank all shift.

#### **OCR and Document Intelligence**

Extracts structured data from invoices, forms, IDs, and scanned records with multi-language support. Turns paper-based workflows into searchable, queryable data without manual re-keying.

#### **Visual Quality Inspection**

Spots defects, anomalies, and out-of-spec products on production lines faster and more consistently than manual inspection. Flags issues before they reach the next stage of the line.

#### **Facial and Biometric Recognition**

Supports identity verification and access control use cases, engineered with the EU AI Act's high-risk classification for biometric systems built in from the design stage, not bolted on after a compliance review.

### **Our Computer Vision Development Process** 

01 Discovery & Business Analysis   
 We assess whether your problem actually needs computer vision or whether a simpler rules-based system solves it faster and cheaper.

02 Data Strategy and Annotation  
 We map your available image and video sources, evaluate quality gaps, and build the annotation pipeline before any model training starts.

03 Model Development and Training  
 We train and fine-tune models against your real conditions, not a clean benchmark dataset that won't survive contact with your floor.

04 Integration and Deployment  
 We connect model output to your existing cameras, ERP, MES, or EHR systems so results trigger action instead of sitting in a dashboard.

05 Monitoring and Retraining  
 We track accuracy after launch and retrain against drift, because a model that isn't monitored degrades quietly until someone notices the wrong way.

### **What does a computer vision development engagement include?**

A computer vision project includes discovery, data preparation, model development, training, integration, deployment, and ongoing monitoring. We build scalable AI vision solutions designed to perform reliably in real-world production environments.

### **How much image or video data is required to train a computer vision model?**

Data requirements depend on the use case and model complexity. Using transfer learning and foundation models, many solutions can be trained effectively with a few thousand well-annotated images.

### **Can you integrate computer vision solutions with our existing systems?**

Yes. We integrate computer vision models with existing cameras, ERP, MES, CRM, EHR, cloud platforms, and custom applications through secure APIs and scalable deployment architectures.

### **What happens after the computer vision model is deployed?**

After deployment, we monitor model performance, identify accuracy drift, retrain models with new data, and optimize performance to ensure reliable, long-term results in production environments.

### **How long does it take to develop a custom computer vision solution?**

A proof of concept typically takes 2–4 weeks, while a production-ready computer vision solution usually requires 8–12 weeks, depending on project complexity, data readiness, and integration requirements.
